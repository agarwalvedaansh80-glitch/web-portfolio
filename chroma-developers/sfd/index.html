The studio

A creative lab, born in Florence

SUPERFUTUREDESIGN* is the sister studio of ASZarchitetti, founded by Andrea Sensoli in Florence. In 2008 the practice opened its branch in the Middle East, today based in Dubai.

The practice counts around 30 people, half of them dedicated to the creative branch. They are BIM natives and master all 3D modelling and visualisation in house, supported by the technical architects and engineers of ASZ Architetti.

    The partners

    Andrea Sensoli & partners

    Andrea Sensoli, Architect Principal

    Rooted in the Florentine School, Andrea graduated with honours in 1993 under prof. Pier Luigi Spadolini. After an apprenticeship with Studio Archea and Claudio Nardi he started his own practice, with clients including Gucci, Sergio Rossi, Yves Saint Laurent and Alexander McQueen. In 2008 he opened the Middle East branch, and today supervises both the ASZ and SFD* teams.

    Iacopo Mannelli, Partner

    A civil engineer from the University of Florence with studies in architecture at the University of Strathclyde, Iacopo has led retail, office, commercial and residential projects for luxury brands. Partner since 2012, he is General Manager of the Milan and Florence offices.

    Andrea Rettori, Partner and Art Director

    An architect by training and a specialist in digital representation and rendering, Andrea joined ASZ in 2006, became a partner in 2012 and is Art Director on all projects worldwide.
