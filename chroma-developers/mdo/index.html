The studio

Matt and Miguel

Creative Director, Matt

Matt has built the business around his passion for design, and his work encompasses every aspect of the outdoor environment. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Environmental Design from the University of Quebec in Montreal and has more than a decade of experience on landscape projects across North America and the Middle East, from concept through to construction.

Operations, Miguel

Miguel studied Building Design and Project Management and has a 15 year background in Europe, South Asia and the Middle East, with notable residential and commercial landscape projects in Dubai and Abu Dhabi since 2008. He leads the MDO team through the technical and execution side of every landscape and pool.

Our clients

We take great pride in our client relationships, evidenced by the many repeat customers who return to us for their next projects. At MDO, we consider it a privilege to bring your outdoor dreams to life, one project at a time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you design and build?

Yes. MDO is a full-service landscape and pool company. We design the garden and our own construction team builds it.

Do you build swimming pools?

Yes. We design and build custom pools for villas, from infinity and overflow pools to sunken lounges and resort-style water features.

What size of project do you take on?

Projects of every scale, from intimate rooftop gardens to expansive residential estates.

Can you renovate an existing garden?

Yes. Many of our projects transform existing villas and beach houses, from a new arrival and driveway to a complete garden and pool.

Do you design outdoor kitchens?

Yes. We design custom outdoor kitchens, barbecue and bar areas and pool houses that integrate with the architecture of your home.
