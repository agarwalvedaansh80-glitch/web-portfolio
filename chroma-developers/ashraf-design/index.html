    What you receive

    Everything included in a full design project

    From the first meeting to the last detail on site. Here is exactly what you get, step by step.

    01

    Brief and measurements

    We meet and discuss your lifestyle, family, taste and budget, and I measure the property or review your existing plans. You receive a written design brief that guides the whole project.

    02

    Space planning

    2–3 layout options with furniture arrangement and zoning. We choose the best one together, so every room works for the way you live.

    03

    Concept and moodboards

    A moodboard for each key space: style direction, colour palette, materials, textures and reference furniture. You see the character of your home before any 3D work begins.

    04

    Photorealistic 3D visualization

    Every room shown from several angles, with real materials, furniture and lighting. Two rounds of revisions are included, so the final images match exactly what you want.

    05

    Working drawings

    A complete set your contractor can build from: demolition and construction plans, furniture layout, ceiling plans, lighting and switch plans, electrical and socket plans, flooring and tiling layouts, plumbing layout, wall elevations, custom joinery drawings, and door and window schedules.

    06

    Specifications

    A full list of every material, finish, furniture piece, light fitting and sanitary item, with codes, suppliers and quantities. Your contractor knows exactly what to order, and you stay in control of the budget.

    07

    Furniture and materials selection

    I help you choose stone, tiles, fabrics, furniture and lighting, in showrooms or from samples, so everything matches the approved design.

    08

    Design supervision

    Weekly site visits, answers to your contractor's questions and quality checks against the drawings, until the project is complete.

    In the end you receive

    A complete design album in PDF, all 3D images in high resolution, and the full drawing set ready for your contractor.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does interior design cost?

    Design fees are calculated per square metre and depend on the package you choose. You can see starting prices in the Pricing section and get a personal quote after sending your floor plan.

    How is the price calculated?

    Design fees are calculated per square metre of the area being designed, depending on the service package. Large or very simple projects may be quoted differently after we discuss the details.

    Does the price include furniture, materials or construction?

    No. The prices cover design and visualization work. Furniture, materials and construction are paid separately to suppliers and contractors.

    Can I order only some rooms?

    Yes. You can start with key spaces such as the living room, majlis or master bedroom, and continue with the rest of the home later.

    How long does a design project take?

    An apartment usually takes about 10–15 working days, and a villa about 20–25 working days on average. The exact schedule depends on the size and scope, and I confirm it in your proposal before we start.

    How many revisions are included?

    Two rounds of revisions are included. We adjust the layout, materials and visualizations together until you are happy with the design.

    How does payment work?

    The payment schedule is set out in your proposal and linked to the stages of the project, so you always pay for clear, visible progress.

    Do you work with contractors?

    Yes. I can work with your own contractor or recommend trusted ones. I prepare working drawings they can build from and supervise on site, so the result matches the approved design.

    How do I get an exact quote?

    Send your floor plan or the approximate area and a few references on WhatsApp. I will review them and send you a personal quote.

    What do I need to send to get started?

    A floor plan or the approximate area, a few photos of the space if it already exists, references you like and your rough budget and timeline. If you don't have a plan yet, we can start from a consultation.

    Can we work together if I live abroad?

    Yes. Most decisions can be made through video calls, WhatsApp and shared visualizations, so you can follow your UAE project from anywhere.
