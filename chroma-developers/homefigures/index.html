Our designer

Lea Chatila

Founded by interior and furniture designer Lea Chatila, Home Figures is a furniture design house born and based in the UAE, with its own production facilities.

Born and raised in Beirut, Lea likes to experiment with shapes and colours, balancing functionality and creativity while maintaining comfort. Her focus is to understand what each client needs, their aspirations and style, and translate it into a unique creation.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Can I create a piece from scratch?

    Yes. Home Figures transforms ideas into reality, from a side table to a DJ stand or a bar. You select the sizes, materials and colours, and our designer takes you through every step.

    How long does a custom order take?

    Custom orders typically take between two and five weeks. The exact timeframe is confirmed for each piece.

    How can I see a material or colour?

    Visit our showroom to view material and colour samples, or request a delivery of samples for a nominal fee, credited back when you place your order.

    Do you deliver outside Dubai?

    Yes. Delivery is free within Dubai, with delivery across the UAE and to Saudi Arabia. For other countries, please contact us.

    Is assembly included?

    Yes. Our team handles all assembly and installation.

    Do you work with interior designers?

    Yes. Our Trade Program offers interior designers special pricing, priority support, customisation options, previews of new collections and fast track delivery.
