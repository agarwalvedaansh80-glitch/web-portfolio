The studio

Architecture closer to everyone

Shawa Architecture, founded by Amer Shawa, specialises in architecture, engineering and design. We believe great design is planned strategically for maximum function while staying focused on aesthetic value.

Our vision is to bring life to our clients' aspirations, and our mission is to guide them through the creative journey with our expertise, delivering every project to the highest standards. See the Dubai Hills Villa, built in 2022.
