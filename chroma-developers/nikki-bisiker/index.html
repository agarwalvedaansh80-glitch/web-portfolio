The studio

Artistry and psychology

A design studio working at the intersection of artistry and psychology. We believe environments shape the people who inhabit them: light, space, proportion and material affect the way a room feels.

A fully integrated design house handling every stage, from concept through architecture, interior design, FF&E, custom furniture and procurement to turnkey delivery. The aim is straightforward: clients arrive at a finished home with a suitcase and start living in it.

    Proportion  ·  Materiality  ·  Restraint

    Designed to be lived in

    How a family moves through a home, how a couple entertains, how a client wants to feel when they walk through the door. These are not afterthoughts; they are the brief.

    Interior architecture

    Drawn from the inside out

    Spatial planning, structural intent, light and circulation are all drawn before surfaces and finishes are considered. We work in plan, elevation and section.

    Services

    From concept to reality

    Full interior design and interior architecture services for discerning clients globally, with a complete first-class service from the first idea to the final accessory.

    I

    Interior Architecture

    Architecture, drawn from the inside out.

    Working alongside architects, engineers and developers, the studio takes responsibility for wall layouts, ceiling treatments, floor levels, integrated joinery, lighting positions and acoustics, so interiors feel resolved rather than decorated.

    • Spatial planning and floor layouts
    • Ceiling and lighting plans
    • Integrated joinery and storage
    II

    Interior Design & FF&E

    Interiors with architectural discipline: proportion, materiality, restraint.

    We test materials before we commit to them and coordinate with the architecture rather than against it. Furniture, lighting, textiles and accessories are sourced, specified and placed with considered precision.

    • Concept and design direction
    • Material, finish and lighting design
    • FF&E specification and styling
    III

    Custom Furniture

    Designed, not selected.

    For more than two decades the studio has designed furniture that grows out of the projects themselves, made with workshops in the UAE, the UK and Italy, each maker chosen for the piece.

    • From a single console to a full suite
    • Timber, stone, leather, metal, lacquer
    • Drawn to the room it will live in
    IV

    Landscape & Project Management

    Outside as a room, and one studio running the whole project.

    Garden, pool, hardscape and planting are coordinated with the architecture. Beyond design, the studio runs the trades, the schedule, the budget and the suppliers, so the design approved at concept is the design that is built.

    • Landscape, pool and outdoor lighting
    • Trade, supplier and site supervision
    • Snagging, handover and aftercare
    Our approach

    Eight phases, one house

    From first conversation to final styling, the studio that designs the project is the studio that delivers it.

    1. 01Concept Design

      The creative vision, spatial direction and design intent, drawn from the way the client lives.

    2. 02Design Development

      The approved concept translated into a coordinated, detailed design.

    3. 03Technical Design

      Drawings and specifications resolved for construction.

    4. 04Budgetary Detailing

      Costs set against every decision before procurement begins.

    5. 05Procurement

      Furniture, lighting and finishes sourced and ordered by the studio.

    6. 06Design Supervision

      On site supervision so the design intent holds through the build.

    7. 07Turnkey Execution

      Contractor coordination through to a complete, finished home.

    8. 08Styling & Final

      The final layer, so the keys hand over a home ready to be lived in.

    How we design

    Every project starts with the room

    01

    The people

    How a family moves through a home, how a couple entertains, how a client wants to feel when they walk through the door. These are the brief.

    02

    The architecture

    How a room sits within the plan, how it connects to the next, how light enters at different times of day. Answered before any finish is chosen.

    03

    The materials

    Timber, stone, leather, metal, lacquer, chosen for how each ages in the room, and tested before we commit to them.

    04

    The delivery

    One team from first sketch to final handover, so the original intent never erodes through translation.

    Philosophy

    Personal and unique to the owner

    We do not impose a signature aesthetic or follow trends. Each project is shaped by a thorough understanding of how the space will be used, who will inhabit it, and what materials and finishes will endure.

    The founder

    Nikki Bisiker

    Nikki grew up around the objects that would shape her career: antiques, fabrics, glassware and fashion collected by her grandparents.

    She designed and project managed restaurants and hotels in the UK in the 1980s, took commissions in Lyon and Monaco through the 1990s, including yacht interiors on the Côte d'Azur, and founded the studio in Dubai in 2004.

    Today she leads the creative direction of every project, drawing on research into how light, layout and material affect the way we feel in a room.
