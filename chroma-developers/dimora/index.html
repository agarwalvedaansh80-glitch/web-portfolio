The result is a home that feels calm, warm and comfortable, with classic and contemporary pieces that sit naturally together. See the award-winning Grand Bleu apartment.

The founder

Deman Sabah

Every project, personally

Dimora Interiors was founded in 2021 and is led by Deman Sabah. She meets each client, visits each property, develops the design, directs the 3D visualisation and joinery details, coordinates suppliers and contractors on site, and styles the finished home herself.

Engineering and interior design

Deman's background brings engineering and interior design together: what can move, what can open up and what will last are decided with the building in mind.

The right specialists

For each project she brings in the specialists it needs, from visualisers and joinery fabricators to contractors and suppliers she knows and trusts.

Where we work

Our studio is at Upside by SRG, Business Bay. Most of our work is in Dubai and the UAE, with selected projects abroad, including corporate offices in Baghdad.
