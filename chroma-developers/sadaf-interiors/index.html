    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Do you handle both design and construction?

    Yes. We design, manage and build, from the first consultation and design through manufacturing, installation and handover.

    Do you make furniture and joinery yourselves?

    Yes. Our factory in Dubai Investment Park has joinery, upholstery, metal, paint, glass and aluminium divisions.

    Which kinds of projects do you take on?

    Villa renovation and decor, offices, retail shops, restaurants, bars, gyms, spas, clinics, showrooms and hotels.

    Will I see the design before work starts?

    Yes. We prepare 3D renderings and visualisation, together with material and finish selections, for your approval.

    How do I start?

    Call or WhatsApp us, or send the form below. We will arrange a free consultation to understand your space and requirements.
