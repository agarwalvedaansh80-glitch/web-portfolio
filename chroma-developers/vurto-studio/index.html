Services

Design, render, film

Three disciplines from one studio: interiors designed with soul, photoreal images built for impact, and films that let a project be felt before it is built.

01

Interior Design

Designs with soul, where story meets refined style to create interiors that feel intentional, elevated and personal.

Each project begins with a clear vision, translated into refined, functional interiors shaped by individuality and character, ending with a complete, execution ready design package.

  • Concept development and moodboards
  • Space planning for Dubai's modern luxury lifestyle
  • Materials and FF&E curated with character
  • High-end visualization before execution
02

3D Rendering

Refined, photorealistic renderings of luxury interiors and architecture, designed for impact.

Every image is built from your drawings, references and design intent. Proportions, surfaces and finishes are represented faithfully, and advanced post-production gives each image the depth and realism of a photograph.

  • Interior and exterior rendering
  • Night and day scenes
  • Clear review stages and revisions
  • High resolution for marketing, tenders and launches
03

Animation

Architectural concepts turned into cinematic journeys, revealing atmosphere, scale and character before a project is built.

Storyboarding and creative direction shape the story, pacing and key moments. Realistic camera movement, sound design and editing turn raw animation into a finished film.

  • Storyboarding and camera planning
  • Realistic environment, lighting and sound
  • Short teasers to full walkthroughs
  • HD or 4K delivery

FAQ

Frequently asked

Have a project in mind? Send your drawings and references and we will shape the vision with you.

What services do you provide?

We specialise in interior design, custom 3D renders and animations. Whether you need a realistic interior render or an animation showcasing your design, we deliver tailored solutions for your project.

Can you create 3D visualizations for exterior designs as well?

Yes. We provide 3D visualizations not only for interiors but also for exterior designs, including architectural facades, landscapes and more.

How do you ensure the quality of your work?

Our artists use 3ds Max and other industry standard software, and every image goes through clear review stages. Every render includes free revisions at each stage, so the final result meets your expectations.

How much does a render cost?

Interior renders are guided at AED 3,500 to 9,500 per image and exterior renders from AED 4,500 per image, depending on scale and detail. Packages of 3 to 5 renders receive 10% off, and 6 or more receive 15% off.

How long can an animation be?

From a 15 to 30 second base animation to a full project film of 60 to 120 seconds, delivered in HD or 4K.
