The founder

Cyril Barret

A French master craftsman trained with the Compagnons du Devoir, Cyril built the company on a deep respect for traditional craftsmanship and hands-on expertise.

Our team is composed of highly qualified craftsmen, fully trained in their respective disciplines, working under the direct guidance of the founder. Every piece is produced in house using hand crafting techniques carefully adapted to the demands of modern, large scale projects.
