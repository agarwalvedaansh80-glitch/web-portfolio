    Services

    From first sketch to handover

    Interior design, fit-out, furniture and lighting from one team, for private clients, developers and operators across the UAE.

    I

    Interior Design & Fit‑Out

    Interiors that reflect the individuality of each client, carried from the first layout to the final finish.

    Our designers and engineers work side by side, so every drawing is planned with the way it will be built in mind.

    • Private villas and residences
    • Building entrances and lobbies
    • Offices, clinics and retail
    II

    Furniture & Joinery

    Bespoke furniture, wall panelling and joinery produced in our own factory in Umm Al Quwain.

    Reception desks, media walls, wardrobes and sleeping pods are made to measure and installed by our team.

    • Reception desks and counters
    • Wall panelling and media walls
    • Wardrobes, storage and display
    III

    Lighting

    Light that shapes the room: sculptural chandeliers, coffered ceilings and concealed lines of light.

    From crystal installations in building lobbies to the glow of a hospitality venue, lighting is planned with the interior from the start.

    • Feature chandeliers and installations
    • Coffered and lit ceilings
    • Concealed and linear lighting
    IV

    Hospitality & Food and Beverage

    Restaurants, cafés and hospitality spaces with a clear identity and hard-working detail.

    We bring together layout, joinery, lighting and finishes so guests feel the idea from the storefront to the last table.

    • Restaurants and cafés
    • Hotels and hospitality
    • Kiosks and mall units
    Our approach

    Every space begins with a vision

    01

    Consultation

    We start by listening: the space, the way you want to use it and the feeling you want it to give.

    02

    Design

    Layouts, materials, lighting and joinery come together into a design you can see clearly before work begins.

    03

    Manufacture

    Furniture and joinery are produced in our factory in Umm Al Quwain, so quality is controlled at the source.

    04

    Execution & Handover

    Our engineers lead the fit-out on site through to a finished space, delivered with precision and careful finishing.
