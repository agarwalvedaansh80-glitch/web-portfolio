The atelier

European design, made in Dubai

Founded in 2019 by Irish designer Sharon O'Keeffe, The Design House merges European craftsmanship with the finesse of Dubai's skilled artisans.

Two areas of expertise sit under one roof: a curated collection available online, and bespoke made-to-order furniture. Every piece can be fully customised, with our team on hand to guide you at each step.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Can I change the size or fabric of a piece?

    Yes. Every item can be fully customised in dimensions, fabric, colour and finish.

    How long does a bespoke piece take?

    We are able to complete bespoke pieces from the first sketch to completion and delivery in less than 8 weeks.

    Can I see fabrics before I order?

    Yes. Fabric swatches can be ordered, and our full range can be seen at the showroom.

    Where is your showroom?

    22nd Street, Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, Dubai. Call us to book an appointment with the team.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, with free shipping on all Dubai orders.
