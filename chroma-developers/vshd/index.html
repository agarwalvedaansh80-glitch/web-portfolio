Founder

Rania Hamed

Interior architect Rania Hamed founded VSHD Design as a boutique practice based in Dubai and Montréal. Her interiors are never led by trend. They are made for timelessness and longevity.

Each project begins with a strong design idea and is carried through with great attention to detail. Light is used as a material, shaping rooms that are as functional as they are quiet. She is drawn to the challenge of bringing traditional culture and technique into contemporary design, and that tension runs through the studio's work.

I believe the best creative decisions come from clarity, not noise.

Rania Hamed, in Harper's Bazaar Interiors Arabia

Practice

What the studio does.

  1. 01

    Architecture

    New houses and pavilions conceived from the plot inward, with landscape, light and interior drawn as one.

  2. 02

    Interior architecture

    Transforming and repurposing existing space for private residences, restaurants, retail, wellness and the workplace.

  3. 03

    Materials and furnishing

    Selection of finishes, furniture, art and decorative pieces, mixing collected and contemporary work with a curator's eye.

  4. 04

    Bespoke design

    Lighting, seating and joinery designed by the studio for a single room, made by trusted workshops.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    A turnkey approach, from first concept and construction documents to coordination on site, so the design intent arrives intact.
