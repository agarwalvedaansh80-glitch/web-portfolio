    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Can you make furniture to my own design?

    Yes. We make custom furniture according to your design, size and requirements. Send us a drawing, a photo or a reference and we will work from it.

    Can you change the size, fabric or colour of a piece I see here?

    Yes. Every piece is made to order, so the size, fabric, colour and finish can be chosen for your space.

    How do I get a quotation?

    Message us on WhatsApp at +971 52 614 1684 with your idea, a photo or the size of your room. The quotation and custom design are free.

    Do you deliver outside Sharjah?

    Yes. We are based in Sharjah and serve customers across the UAE, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Ajman and all Emirates, with free home delivery.

    Do you furnish complete villas and offices?

    Yes. From luxury villas and modern apartments to offices and commercial spaces, we provide furniture, curtains, décor and complete interior solutions.

    Do you also make curtains and wall décor?

    Yes. Alongside furniture we make curtains and window décor, wall décor, feature walls and gypsum work.
