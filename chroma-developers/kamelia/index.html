    Unique, tailored and lived in

    Every garden starts with how you wish to use it: the shade you need, the colours and textures you love, the hours you will spend outside.

    Services

    Complete landscape design packages

    From the first meeting through to tender documentation, and on site until the garden is handed over.

    I

    Landscape Design

    A landscape plan shaped around your brief, with 3D renders and mood boards.

    Material selection, planting palette, lighting look and feel, garden accessories and furniture, refined with you into a final landscape masterplan.

    II

    Detailed Design & Documentation

    Every detail drawn, specified and ready for contractors.

    Hard and soft landscape plans, levels, sections and elevations, lighting and irrigation, pool and water features, pergolas and outdoor kitchens, plus specifications and a Bill of Quantities.

    III

    Site Supervision & Administration

    We stay on site until the garden matches the design.

    Tender support and analysis, then supervision of the landscape contractor so the work meets the design and your expectations, with clear reports and recommendations.

    Our process

    A clear path to your garden

    01

    Meeting & brief

    We listen to your requirements, your preferred style and the planting colours and textures you love, and how you wish to use the garden. Then we write the brief together.

    02

    Fee proposal

    A clear proposal with every design stage explained, its deliverables and time scales.

    03

    Site survey & analysis

    We assess the site and its surroundings and gather the technical information we need before design begins.

    04

    Design & masterplan

    A landscape plan with 3D renders and mood boards, refined with you into the final landscape masterplan.

    05

    Detailed design & tender

    Construction drawings, specifications and a Bill of Quantities, then support through tender with a clear recommendation.

    06

    Site supervision

    We supervise the landscape works so the finished garden matches the design and your expectations.

    The lead designer

    Kamelia Zaal

    Founder and Lead Designer

    Kamelia has worked in landscape design for over fifteen years. In 2006 she established Second Nature landscape design and began work on Al Barari, the UAE's most distinguished, luxurious and sustainable green residential development, work that won her no fewer than three awards.

    A first at Chelsea

    In 2015 Kamelia became the first Arab landscape designer ever to take part in the RHS Chelsea Flower Show, where her garden won a Silver Gilt Medal. In 2016 she won a Silver Gilt Medal at the Gardening World Cup.
