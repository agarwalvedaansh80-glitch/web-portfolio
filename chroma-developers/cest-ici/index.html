About

Monica Arango

Founder and Creative Director

Monica Arango is the founder and creative director of C'est ici Design, an award-winning multidisciplinary studio recognised for its distinctive approach to luxury interior design and interior architecture.

Emotional minimalism

Her philosophy is rooted in emotional minimalism, creating timeless spaces through refined simplicity, sculptural forms and natural materiality, balanced with warmth and individuality.

Spaces made to belong

More than creating beautiful interiors, the studio designs places to live, gather, connect and belong, spaces with meaning, made to be experienced and remembered.

FAQ

Before we begin

What kind of projects does the studio take on?

Luxury residential, hospitality, retail and commercial projects in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and internationally, from single spaces to complete turnkey villas.

Do you design bespoke furniture?

Yes. Bespoke design is part of every project, and the Inverti collection, a collaboration with Dana Mahlas, includes side tables, consoles, benches, stools, planters and candle holders.

Can you deliver a villa turnkey?

Yes. Projects such as Marrakech on Jubail Island were delivered as turnkey commissions, from interior architecture to FF&E and styling.
