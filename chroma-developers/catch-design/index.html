About the studio

Great interior design is our passion

Your space should show off your taste and personality, work with your lifestyle, and maybe make your guests a little jealous. Creating a home that does all of this isn't easy, and we're here to help.

Catch Design is a full service interior and architecture studio based in Dubai, with a second office in Kyiv. From new villas to the renovation of existing homes, through to high end interior production, a qualified team plans and furnishes luxurious living spaces for clients around the world.

What we do

Full service design, inside and out

Interior, landscape, architecture and fit-out solutions, planned and delivered by one qualified team, anywhere in the world.

01

Interior Design

Photorealistic 3D design and a complete set of working drawings for every room.

Zoning with a furniture layout, photorealistic 3D renders and drawings your contractor can build from.

  • 3D design and photorealistic renders
  • Zoning and furniture layout
  • Ceiling, lighting, electrical and plumbing plans
  • Furniture, lighting and finishes specifications
02

Exterior Design

The house itself: master plan, facades and photorealistic exterior views.

A master plan with furniture layout, up to two options, developed into the full architectural set.

  • Master plan, floor plans and roof plan
  • Elevations and sections of the building
  • Finishes, door and window schedules
03

Landscape

Gardens, terraces and pools planned as a continuation of the interior.

A landscape plan for the house with up to two planning options, a master plan and 3D visualisation.

  • Up to two planning options
  • Landscape master plan
  • 3D visualisation
04

Project Management

Selection, deliveries and site control, so the result matches the design.

We look after the details between the drawings and the finished home.

  • Selection and calculation of specifications
  • Organisation and control of deliveries
  • Monitoring construction and all contractors
  • Acceptance of materials and furniture
05

Fit-out & Implementation

From new construction and renovation to high end interior production.

A full service from planning and design through to the finished, furnished home, delivered by a qualified team.

  • New spaces and renovation of existing ones
  • High end interior production
  • Luxurious, fully furnished living spaces
