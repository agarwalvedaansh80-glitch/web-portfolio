The studio

Elegant, timeless, couture

Hill House Interiors is the fruit of a 25-year friendship between its founding partners, Jenny Weiss and Helen Bygraves, built on a shared belief that beautiful surroundings can transform people's lives.

Their client-centred approach has garnered a multitude of awards, while the business has grown into one of Britain's leading interior design practices. Hill House operates from a creative studio and lifestyle showroom in the UK, and a luxurious office on Sheikh Zayed Road in Dubai.

Services

From concept to reality

Full interior design and interior architecture services for discerning clients globally, with a complete first-class service from the first idea to the final accessory.

I

Interior Design

Personalised, elegant and infinitely varied in scope, resulting in environments that are truly unique to each client.

Rather than stamping a one-size-fits-all aesthetic onto every space, the Hill House signature is more discreet: a philosophy that considers all the senses, from movement through space to scale, light, materials, colours, texture and even sound.

  • Exclusive penthouse apartments
  • Palatial homes and lakeside villa retreats
  • Beautifully engineered yachts
II

Interior Architecture

Architecture and interiors conceived as a single, cohesive vision, with our in-house architects or through creative collaboration.

Many of our projects require architectural expertise. From reconfigured layouts that maximise space and light to bespoke cornices, sculptural staircases and restored period features, every space is shaped around the way our clients live.

  • Spatial planning and reconfiguration
  • Extensions and full refurbishments
  • Restoration of original period features
III

Project Management

Our design and architecture team oversee the entire project from concept to installation, on time and on budget.

We attend regular site meetings to ensure our designs are achieved to the exact specifications. Effective management of the manufacture process and strong relationships with trusted suppliers result in a seamless transition through to installation.

  • Regular site meetings
  • Manufacture and supplier management
  • Seamless installation
IV

Turn-key & Styling

We believe that home accessories are like the jewellery to an outfit.

We offer full turn-key and styling services. Our Lifestyle Showroom is a taste of Hill House luxury, with new products, accessories, beautiful objects, floristry and artworks, and a team who design individual bespoke pieces.

  • Full turn-key delivery
  • Accessories, artworks and floristry
  • Individual bespoke pieces
Our approach

It all starts with an idea

01

The Brief

From our initial meeting, we take time to learn about the client and their design ambitions, exploring the creative, financial and practical feasibility of the site or concept to achieve the finest outcome.

02

Design Development

With the brief at the forefront, our expertise in spatial planning and an understanding of both client and property, we refine the initial concepts, sourcing furniture and finishes with a trained eye and discoveries from international travel.

03

Presentation

We communicate our proposals through tactile mood boards, technical drawings and visualisations, so our clients can truly visualise the final outcome.

04

Project Coordination

Our team oversee the entire project from concept to installation, attending regular site meetings so every design is achieved to the exact specifications, on time and on budget.

Philosophy

A stage set and a backdrop to life

Central to the Hill House way of working is a seize-the-day ethos that celebrates living life to the full. We encourage clients to be surrounded by the things they cherish, and use these personal references as a point of departure for harmonious, uplifting and timeless environments.

Dubai studio

A British design house in Dubai

Hill House Interiors has opened a global design consultancy with a prominent office in the heart of Dubai, bringing award-winning British design to homes and apartments across the UAE.

Our directors have strong personal and professional connections to the Middle East, and an affinity with Dubai in particular. With a passion for the market and a strong understanding of the intricacies of high-end design, we design, plan, develop and manage luxury projects from initial conception through to completion.

Our style reflects high-end, bespoke British design, blending the top-quality craftsmanship the UK is known for with a flair for the extravagant that is so fitting of the Dubai culture.

Our directors

Jenny Weiss & Helen Bygraves

Founders, for over twenty five years

Launching their multi-award-winning design practice over twenty five years ago, Jenny and Helen are the founders of Hill House Interiors. Having both worked within the property sector, their Surrey based design studio has flourished.

A team of over thirty

Today, with a team of over 30 designers, architects and craftspeople, their enthusiasm for high-end couture interiors coupled with their positive energy brings their diverse projects to life.

Personally involved in every project

We lead by example, personally and passionately involved in every project, inspiring each other and the talented design team to create exceptional interiors that surpass client expectations.

From exclusive penthouse apartments to palatial homes, lakeside villa retreats and beautifully engineered yachts, Hill House works on high-end residential projects in locations as diverse as London, New York, Lake Como, Antibes, the Bahamas, Ibiza, Los Angeles and Dubai.
