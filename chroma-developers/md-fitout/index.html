    Our story

    From a joinery workshop to turnkey design and build

    1. 2013

      The beginning

      MD Design & Fit-Out starts as a small joinery workshop in Ras Al Khor, driven by craftsmanship and custom interiors.

    2. 2018

      A multidisciplinary team

      Architects, interior designers, engineers and project managers join the team.

    3. 2019

      Dedicated joinery division

      Bespoke furniture, woodwork and cabinetry produced in house, with greater quality control.

    4. 2021

      Turnkey design and build

      Services expand to complete design-build, MEP, civil works and renovation.

    5. 2024

      1,500+ projects

      Residential, commercial, hospitality, healthcare, education and retail projects across Dubai and the UAE.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you provide turnkey fit-out?

    Yes. We manage the whole process, from design, planning and authority approvals to construction, MEP, joinery, finishing and final handover.

    Which projects do you take on?

    Villas, apartments, offices, retail stores, restaurants and cafés, hospitality venues, healthcare facilities and commercial spaces across Dubai and the UAE.

    Do you make your own joinery?

    Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes, doors, wall panels and bespoke furniture are produced in our own joinery workshop in Ras Al Khor.

    Can you handle authority approvals?

    Yes. Depending on the project we coordinate approvals, permits and NOCs with Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence, DEWA, Trakhees and building management.

    Will I see the design before work begins?

    Yes. We offer 3D visualisation and space planning so you can see the outcome before execution.

    Which areas do you serve?

    All Emirates, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.
