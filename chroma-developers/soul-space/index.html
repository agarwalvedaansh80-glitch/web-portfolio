The studio

A house becomes a home

Soul Space is a full service interior design studio that designs spaces from concept to completion in Dubai and India, for new builds and renovations.

Our strength is remote project management: we take full responsibility for executing a project, even for clients in other cities or countries, with minimal input needed from them.

    Quick Fix

    A quick and easy way to refresh a space.

    For rentals and tight timelines: expert guidance on furniture placement, lighting and new accessories that transform a room without a full renovation.

    • Furniture placement
    • Lighting design
    • Accessories and styling
    III

    Remote Project Management

    Full responsibility for your project, wherever you are.

    • One point of contact

    • Execution with minimal input from you

    Founder & creative head

    Sunayana

    Born and raised in India, Sunayana's passion for interior design began right after school, before a degree in finance and an eight year career in Dubai.

    During the pandemic she remodelled her own apartment and shared the journey on Instagram. Requests to design other homes followed, and in 2022 she left her corporate job to pursue design full time.

    Today the studio is immersed in projects across Dubai and India, crafting spaces tailored to the needs of each client.
