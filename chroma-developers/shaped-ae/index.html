Our story

From Guildford to Dubai

Shaped began in Guildford, Surrey, and moved to Dubai for the growing demand for high quality bespoke furniture and joinery.

Our in-house joinery factory in Dubai Investment Park 1 runs on Italian machinery for precision and consistency. From the first drawing to installation, you are kept informed at every stage, so what you approve is exactly what is built.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Can every piece be made to my size?

    Yes. Our furniture and joinery are fully bespoke, so sizes, materials and finishes are tailored to your space.

    Where is your furniture made?

    In our own joinery factory at Street 55, Warehouse 3, Dubai Investment Park 1, Dubai.

    Can you change the stone or the finish of a media wall?

    Yes. Surrounds can be travertine, book matched marble, rock, linen or a hand painted finish, with timber or painted joinery and integrated LED lighting.

    Do you also handle fit-out and renovation?

    Yes. We provide interior design, renovation and fit-out alongside bespoke furniture and joinery.
