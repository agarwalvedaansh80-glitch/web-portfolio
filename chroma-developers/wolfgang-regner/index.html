About

German precision, Dubai living

The founder

Wolfgang Regner is a German architect and designer, born in London and raised between Bombay, Srinagar, Paris and Hamburg. He studied architecture and design in Germany and has worked on private homes in Dubai since 2010.

The studio

Wolfgang Regner Architects is an award winning consultancy in City Walk, Dubai, for architectural, interior, landscape, furniture and engineering design, adhering to the highest international standards.

The approach

Clarity, restraint and purpose: timeless architecture that answers contemporary living and enduring design values, shaped by passion, curiosity, innovation and individuality.

FAQ

Before we begin

What kind of projects does the studio take on?

Mainly medium to large scale private luxury villas, as well as private majlis and clubs, in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Ajman and abroad.

Can you design the interiors and landscape too?

Yes. The studio is an architectural, interior, landscape, furniture and engineering design consultancy, so the whole villa is designed as one.

Do you supervise construction?

Yes. Site supervision and project management are part of the services, alongside structural and MEP design.

How do we start?

Send a short message with your plot location, the size of the villa and your timeline. The studio will reply to arrange a first meeting at City Walk or on site.
