The studio

Defining space, elevating it

Established in 2003, TAG Interiors is a design firm seeking to define space and elevate it to new heights and perspectives.

We believe people's surroundings shape their quality of life. Our designers work on aesthetic designs that are both functional and efficient, with each project keeping its own individuality and every electromechanical detail in dialogue with the design.

    Services

    From the first plan to the final fabric

    A diversity of projects, from private apartments to operations at a much larger scale.

    I

    Design & Fit-Out

    Luxury residences, offices, high-rise buildings, hotels, spas, schools, clinics, hospitals and showrooms.

    Each project keeps its own individuality, with generous, well planned space and a careful dialogue between the design and every electromechanical detail.

    • Residences and villas
    • Offices and high-rise buildings
    • Hotels, spas and clinics
    II

    Bespoke Joinery

    Our joinery arm, TAGline Woodworks, crafts the details that make a space: quality, precision and finish.

    Founded in 2021, TAGline builds things that tell a story, from feature walls and storage to custom pieces made for each project.

    • Feature walls and panelling
    • Kitchens, pantries and storage
    • Custom pieces
    III

    Textile & Furniture

    An innovative line of textiles and bespoke furniture, presented in the MAJALESS showroom on Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai.

    Curtains, upholstery and furniture are selected and made to complete each interior, so every room is finished to the same standard.

    • Bespoke furniture
    • Curtains and textiles
    • Majlis seating
    IV

    Research & Materials

    We follow today's technology and trends to apply the latest creative methods with superior quality materials.

    Considerable research and planning sit behind every scheme, from earth and clay inspired finishes to stone, timber and metal.

    • Material research
    • Lighting and finishes
    • Planning and documentation
    Our approach

    Every space has its own voice

    01

    Listening

    We start with the people who will use the space, how they live or work, and what the space needs to say.

    02

    Planning

    Spatial planning that treats generous, well proportioned space as essential, coordinated with every technical system.

    03

    Design & materials

    Finishes, lighting, textiles and furniture chosen through research, with quality materials and current creative methods.

    04

    Joinery & delivery

    Our joinery and furniture teams bring the details to life, so the finished space matches the design.
