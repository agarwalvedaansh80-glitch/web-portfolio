Our services

From drawings to decisions

From exterior and interior renders to walkthrough animations and 360 panoramas, our 3D rendering services help architects and developers in Dubai turn drawings into decisions. Each service can stand on its own or sit in one visual set for a launch.

01

3D Architectural Rendering

Photoreal exterior stills for villas, towers, and masterplans before a wall is built.

Villas, towers, and masterplans are lit, materialled, and finished until glass, stone, planting, and sky read as a place, not a sketch on a white field. If a still cannot survive a client meeting, it is not finished.

  • Photoreal exterior stills for villas, towers, and plots
  • Day, dusk, and night cameras from the same model
  • Wide site views and close facade crops
  • Files ready for print, web, and presentations
02

360 Panorama

Full surround stills so clients can look around a space, not only at a single camera.

The frame is equirectangular, but the standard is the same as a hero render: material, light, and a space that feels finished. Headsets, web players, and gallery screens can all take the same file.

  • Equirectangular stills with photoreal material and light
  • Interior, amenity, and exterior cameras
  • Files for web tours, sales galleries, and headsets
  • The same finish as the hero stills in the set
03

3D Interior Renderings

Interior stills with material, light, and atmosphere ready to approve.

Living rooms, lobbies, suites, and amenity interiors are furnished and lit so material, daylight, and atmosphere feel finished before production. Stakeholders read the space as a photograph, not as a drawing they have to imagine.

  • Photoreal interiors for living rooms, lobbies, and suites
  • Furniture, material, and daylight finished for approval
  • Day and mood cameras for sales and design review
  • Stills formatted for decks, print, and the web
04

Interior Design Support

Support for design teams testing layout, material, and lighting before production.

This is visualization as a design tool, not only as a marketing still. Two looks of the same volume can sit side by side until the room is signed off, so a fit out team is not guessing on site.

  • Option stills for layout, material, and lighting
  • Variant rooms so a team can compare looks
  • Mood studies before procurement
  • Files that can move from design review into sales
05

AR / VR Visualization

Immersive AR and VR that let buyers step into a project before it is built.

The scenes are prepared for launches, sales galleries, and remote review so the unbuilt space can be walked, not only looked at. We keep the same model, light, and finish across the set.

  • Interactive scenes from the same project model
  • Experiences for launches, galleries, and remote review
  • Interior and exterior environments with matching finish
  • A path from stills and panoramas into immersive view
06

Cinematic walkthroughs that show a project in motion.

We guide the viewer through approach, lobby, rooms, and amenity so the project is felt in time, not only in a still. Cameras, light, and cut are set with the same material standard as the stills.

  • Cinematic walkthroughs for marketing and investor decks
  • A path through approach, lobby, rooms, and amenity
  • The same material finish as the stills in the set
  • Cuts ready for web, presentation, and launch screens

FAQ

3D rendering, answered

Have a project in mind? Send drawings or a model and we will confirm scope, views, and timeline.

What 3D rendering services does Blackwhite Viz offer?

We deliver exterior architectural rendering, interior stills, 360 panoramas, interior design support, walkthrough videos, and AR/VR visualization. Each service can be commissioned on its own or as a full visual set for a launch.

What files do you need to start a rendering project?

CAD or BIM models, drawings, and a brief on camera views, materials, and lighting are enough to begin. Mood references and brand guidelines help us match the look you need. If the model is incomplete, we can rebuild or refine it before lighting.

How long do 3D rendering services take?

A focused stills package often lands in days to a few weeks. Walkthroughs, large masterplans, and AR/VR take longer. After we review scope, we lock a schedule around your presentation or sales date.

Do you work with architects and developers outside Dubai?

Yes. We are based in Dubai and work with teams across the UAE, the Gulf, Africa, and South Asia. Remote reviews and shared folders keep the process clear wherever the site is.

How does architectural visualization differ from traditional blueprints?

Blueprints and CAD drawings are technical. Architectural visualization turns those plans into realistic images and animations so clients can experience scale, lighting, and atmosphere long before construction begins.

Do you provide 3D interior rendering for apartments and villas?

Yes. We produce interior renders for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, and amenity spaces, with material accuracy, furniture styling, and day, dusk, and night lighting options for developers and homeowners.

Can you create 2D and 3D floor plans for a project?

Yes. We deliver 2D plans for quick layout overviews and 3D floor plans that show furniture, circulation, and space flow, effective for brochures, websites, and sales galleries.

What is the difference between exterior and interior rendering?

Exterior rendering covers façades, podiums, street life, and how the building meets the site. Interior rendering focuses on rooms, materials, furniture, and daylight. Most sales and design decks need both so the project reads as a complete place.

Can you create 3D walkthrough videos and AR/VR?

Yes. Walkthroughs take viewers from approach through lobby, rooms, and amenities. AR/VR is used when a client needs to stand inside the design, not only watch it. We advise which format fits the launch.

How do you use 3D rendering for real estate marketing?

We create hero stills, detail crops, animations, and aerial views formatted for brochures, websites, social media, and presentations, so marketing teams have a consistent visual set for every channel.

How do we start a service with Blackwhite Viz?

Send drawings or a model through Contact, or request a price guideline. We confirm scope, views, and timeline, then share a first look for direction before we finish the set.
