Questions

Good to know

Do you carry out the construction?

No. DRProject designs your villa, then oversees the contractors on site. We do not carry out the works ourselves, which keeps the supervision independent.

Who hires the contractors?

Contractors can be hired by us or directly by you. Either way, we oversee them at every stage.

What does the free brief include?

We talk through the site, how you want to live there, the budget and the timing. This first step is free.

Can you design the furniture too?

Yes. Furniture is designed and controlled by the studio and manufactured by our partners, so it matches the interior exactly.

Do you work on apartments and commercial projects?

Yes. Alongside villas, the studio designs premium apartments and commercial spaces.

Will I see the design before construction?

Yes. The visual design stage includes photorealistic visualisations, so you can change decisions before construction starts.
