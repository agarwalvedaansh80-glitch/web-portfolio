Questions before the first roll.

Can I see my photos before they develop?

No, and that's the point. You'll see the counter go down, but every photo stays sealed until the develop time the roll was set to.

What if one of my shots is bad?

For the first hour after the roll develops, you can hide any of your own shots before the rest of the crew saves the roll.

How many people can join a roll?

Up to 12. Everyone gets the same number of shots, set by the person who starts the roll.

Does everyone need the app?

No. Guests can shoot from the invite link in their phone's browser. The app adds film looks and lets you shoot offline.

How much will it cost?

Rolls of up to 27 shots each will be free. Longer rolls and extra film looks will be part of a paid plan, priced before launch.

Who owns the photos?

Whoever took them. Overnight stores the roll so your crew can save it, and the person who started a roll can delete it at any time.
