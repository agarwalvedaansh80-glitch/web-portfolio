Claims · what happens after you call us

Five steps from storm to calm.

00:00 The call

A broker answers. Not a queue.

Ring 0113 496 0499 at any hour. A Calder & Rowe broker picks up, usually inside three rings, and stays your named contact until the claim closes.

Hour 2 Logged

Your claim is with the insurer the same day.

We notify the insurer, send you the claim reference and arrange the emergency help your policy allows: boarding up, drying out, IT incident response.

Day 3 On site

We meet the loss adjuster with you.

We sit in on the visit, help you gather photos, invoices and figures, and make sure nothing you are owed is left off the list.

Week 2 Cash flow

We push for an interim payment.

Rebuilds take months. We negotiate payments on account so wages and suppliers are paid while the claim runs, and we update you every week, even when there is no news.

Week 9 Calm

Settled, line by line.

We check the final settlement against your schedule before you sign, then use what we learned to tighten your cover at renewal.

Typical timings for a mid-sized property claim. Every claim is different, and we will tell you honestly what to expect on yours.
