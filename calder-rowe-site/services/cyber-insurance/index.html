Cyber insurance

Incident response within hours, restored systems, lost income and payment fraud, with help answering the questions insurers ask.

Book a free cover check
Compare the levels

Most cyber incidents at smaller firms start with an email. Cyber insurance pays specialists to contain the attack and restore your systems, and covers the income you lose while you are offline.

What it covers

  • Incident response: a 24-hour helpline, IT forensics and legal advice
  • Data and system restoration after ransomware or a failed attack
  • Business interruption while your systems are down
  • Liability and regulatory costs if personal data is exposed
  • Payment fraud, where staff are tricked into paying a fake invoice

Compare the levels

Every level includes the response team. What changes is the limit, lost income, and how much fake-invoice fraud is covered.

Who it suits

  • Firms that hold customer or employee personal data
  • Businesses that take payments or send invoices by email
  • Companies that cannot trade when their systems go down
  • Suppliers whose contracts require cyber cover

What moves the price

  • Multi-factor authentication on email and remote access
  • Offline backups, and how often you test a restore
  • Turnover and the volume of personal data you hold
  • Patching, staff training and past incidents

An illustrative example

An accountancy practice opens an attachment that appears to come from a client, and ransomware encrypts the file server. The insurer’s response team isolates the network, restores data from backups and handles notifications to clients. The policy pays their fees and three days of lost billing.

A hypothetical scenario to show how the cover works. Not a real client or claim.

Common questions

Do insurers pay ransoms?

Cover for ransom payments varies and some insurers exclude it. Most claims are resolved by restoring from backups, which is why insurers ask so much about them.

We are a small firm. Are we really a target?

Most attacks are automated and indiscriminate. Smaller firms are often hit because they have fewer controls, not because anyone chose them.

What if we do not have multi-factor authentication?

Many insurers will not quote without it. We can tell you which controls matter most and help you close the gaps before renewal.
