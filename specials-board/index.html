Three post types built from things you already do, so you never stare at a blank caption again.

  1. The bake. What came out of the oven, when it lands, when it usually sells out.
  2. The regulars. A name, an order, a line they said. Asked first, always.
  3. The bar. The person making the coffee, the pour, the new bean on the grinder.
  4. Wildcard, once a week. A delivery, a broken mixer, a rainy queue. Real days sell.

Better to know now

Is this the right course for you?

It’s for you if

  • You own or run an independent café, bakery or coffee cart.
  • You’d rather do it yourself than pay an agency every month.
  • You can spare 20 minutes a day and one hour a week.
  • You have a phone from the last five years. That’s the whole kit.

It’s not for you if

  • You want thousands of followers fast. There are no growth hacks here.
  • You run a chain with its own marketing team.
  • You want someone else to post for you.
  • You’re mainly after paid ads. We cover them in one lesson, not six.

Before you join

Questions owners ask.

Something else on your mind? Email Ines. She replies within a working day.

I barely have time to sit down. Is 20 minutes a day realistic?

Yes, because most of it happens in one batch session in week four. Day to day you’re taking a photo during service and posting something already written. If a week goes sideways, the recordings wait for you.

I’m not creative and I’m not a photographer.

You don’t need to be. Week two gives you a shot list and two light spots in your own café. Most owners find it’s a checklist, not a talent.

Do I have to show my face or talk to camera?

No. Every format in the course works with hands, cups and the room. If you’d like to be on camera, there’s an optional lesson for that.

Will this bring more customers in?

We can’t promise a number, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What the course gives you is a steady presence and, in week six, a way to measure which posts lead to visits so you can do more of those.

What if I miss a live call?

Every call is recorded and up within a few hours. Post your questions in the group chat beforehand and Ines will answer them on the recording.

Which phone and apps do I need?

Any iPhone or Android from the last five years, the Instagram app, and a free scheduling tool we set up together. Nothing to buy.

Can a member of staff take the course instead of me?

Yes. Many owners hand Instagram to a barista. The seat is for one person, and they can share the templates with the team.

How does the refund work?

Email within 30 days of the cohort start. No form, no call. The refund goes back to your card within five working days.
