    [ 06 ]Questions dispatchers ask

    Bring your messiest Tuesday.

    Can’t find yours? Book a 20-minute walkthrough and we will replan a real day of yours on the call.

    Book a walkthrough
    Can we keep our current job list?

    Yes. Upload a spreadsheet or connect your booking form and Slotted maps the columns for you. Most teams finish the import in an afternoon.

    Does it handle multi-day installs?

    Yes. Short visits sit on the day board, and installs can span several days while keeping the same crew across them.

    What happens when a tech calls in sick?

    Mark them as off and Slotted offers to move their jobs to techs with matching skills, showing the new drive times before you confirm.

    Do customers need to install anything?

    No. Arrival windows arrive as a text message with a link to a live map. There is nothing to download.

    Can we cancel at any time?

    Monthly plans end at the close of the billing month. Annual plans can be cancelled for a pro-rated refund in the first 30 days.
