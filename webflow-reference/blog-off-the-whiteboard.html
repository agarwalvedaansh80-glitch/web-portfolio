Moving off the whiteboard in two weeks

The magnetic board on the office wall has served you well. Here is a two-week plan to replace it without a single missed visit.

MV
Marisol Vega
Dispatch lead, Slotted
Oct 14, 20264 min read
ON THIS PAGE

Plenty of good service businesses still run on a whiteboard, a set of coloured magnets and one dispatcher who knows where everything is. It works until that dispatcher goes on holiday, or the team grows past a dozen vans, or a customer asks where their technician is and nobody can say.

Switching does not have to be a big-bang weekend project. This is the plan we use with teams moving their first board.

Week one: run both boards

  • Day 1: import your customer list and next two weeks of jobs from a spreadsheet
  • Day 2: add every technician with their skills and certifications
  • Days 3 to 5: keep the whiteboard as the source of truth, and copy each change into the new board at the end of the day

This week is about trust. The dispatcher should see that the new board matches the wall, every evening.

Week two: flip the source of truth

  • Day 6: techs start the day from the app instead of the printed sheet
  • Day 7: new jobs are booked on the new board first and copied to the wall
  • Days 8 to 10: switch on arrival window texts for one crew, then for everyone

By the end of week two the whiteboard is a copy. Leave it up for another week if it helps the team feel safe, then wipe it.

Do not wipe the whiteboard until the people who use it ask you to.

What usually goes wrong

The most common problem is jobs that live only in someone’s head: the regular customer who always gets Tuesday mornings, the site that needs a ladder. Spend an hour with your senior techs in week one and write those down as job notes. They are the real schedule.
