How it’s poured

Slow on purpose.

  1. 01Poured at 62°

    Coconut and rapeseed wax, melted slowly and poured at 62°C so the fragrance binds instead of flashing off. A run is 120 jars, never more.

  2. 02Centred by hand

    A waxed cotton wick, held dead centre by a beech bar while the jar sets. Off-centre wicks tunnel; ours burn flat to the edge.

  3. 03Cured for fourteen days

    Fragrance needs two weeks to marry the wax. We wait, so your first burn smells like the hundredth.
